# Portfolio evidence checklist

Add live-product material only after redacting caller and tenant information.

## Recommended screenshots

1. **Operator dashboard:** call volume, handled outcomes, duration, or cost cards with a visible date range.
2. **Agent configuration:** persona, business hours, supported actions, and phone assignment without private prompt data.
3. **Knowledge ingestion:** completed crawl, source list, or knowledge status without private URLs or content.
4. **Call lifecycle:** synthetic call timings and outcome without real audio, transcript, or caller identity.
5. **Administration:** provider health, latency, queue, or budget controls without credential values.

## Demo recording

Use fictional business data and a test number. Show the configured agent, place a call, ask one grounded question, interrupt the assistant, request an appointment or transfer, then show the resulting synthetic call record.

Remove browser bookmarks, notifications, passwords, provider dashboards, and unrelated tabs before recording.

## Claims rule

Do not publish latency, accuracy, uptime, volume, conversion, or savings figures without a dated source and measurement method.
